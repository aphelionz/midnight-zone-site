The Midnight Zone

A bathypelagic blacklight submersion by Amy Chace.

A thousand meters down, the ocean stops receiving light and starts making its own. Amy Chace paints with UV pigments, so her work does the same thing. In daylight it is one set of paintings. Under blacklight it is another: same board, same brushstrokes, a different picture. There is a booth in Gloucester lit for exactly this, and failing a trip to Cape Ann, there is the switch below.

Come stand in it

The Midnight Zone is a UV-lit booth inside Fish City Studios, 39 Main St, Gloucester, Massachusetts, in Jon Sarkin's last studio. The work hangs there under blacklight, alongside the Sarkin archive.

Amy Chace is a Rockport painter and illustrator who works as the Whimsical Maximalist. She was a band photographer in New York before this, and she has published two oracle decks with Schiffer.
