The Midnight Zone ●

A bathypelagic blacklight submersion

A thousand meters down, the ocean stops receiving light and starts making its own.

Amy Chace paints with UV pigments. In daylight these are one set of paintings. Under blacklight they are another. Same board, same brushstrokes, two pictures. There is a booth in Gloucester lit for exactly this, and people walk in, watch the wall turn on, and then say the same thing every time: I don't have a blacklight room at home.

Come stand in it

The Midnight Zone is a UV-lit booth inside Fish City Studios, 39 Main St, Gloucester, Massachusetts, in Jon Sarkin's last studio. The work hangs there under the light it was made for, alongside the Sarkin archive.

Amy Chace is a Rockport painter and illustrator who works as the Whimsical Maximalist. She was a band photographer in New York before this, and she has published two oracle decks with Schiffer.
